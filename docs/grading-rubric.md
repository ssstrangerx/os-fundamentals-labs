# Grading Rubric

## 1. Process Observation and Linux Commands (20%)
- Correct use of process inspection commands
- Clear explanation of process states and services
- Accurate analysis of live system behavior

## 2. Process Creation and Management (20%)
- Correct understanding of fork/exec patterns
- Explanation of process hierarchy and parent-child relationships
- Accurate interpretation of examples

## 3. CPU Scheduling Simulation (25%)
- Correct implementation or simulation of scheduling algorithms
- Correct metrics calculations
- Comparison and discussion of fairness and efficiency

## 4. Threads and Synchronization (15%)
- Demonstration of shared-memory operations
- Explanation of locks and race conditions
- Safe and correct implementation

## 5. IPC and Communication (15%)
- Clear process communication example
- Explanation of message passing and coordination
- Valid demonstration of cooperation between processes

## 6. Report Quality and Presentation (5%)
- Organization, formatting, and technical correctness
- Reflection on learning outcomes
